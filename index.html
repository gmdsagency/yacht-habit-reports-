6Why it happened

Seller leads lowered cost; follow-up now limits appointments

  • Why leads got cheaper: Cost per lead fell 45% in the week of Oct 19, when the Sellers campaign launched at about $6.75 per lead.
  • Why buyer leads cost more: English buyer ads were the most expensive audience that week at $85.41 per lead, with a $109 cost per 1,000 impressions. FLIBS pushed competition up.
  • Why few appointments: 6 of 22 opportunities are in No Answer and most of the rest are at first contact, so conversations have only started.

7Market and competitors

FLIBS advertising is crowded; Spanish and Russian remain open

  • About 320 ads mentioned FLIBS in Meta by Oct 26, up 78% in a week. None were in Spanish.
  • Autograph Yacht Group launched about 24 ads and appears as sponsored when people search for Yacht Habit in Google Maps.
  • Denison began promoting price reductions, a new angle for yacht sales.
  • Opportunity: no competitor sells yachts in Spanish or Russian in Meta ads.
